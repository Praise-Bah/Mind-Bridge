# Campus Facilities at ICT University

## Location

ICT University is located in Yaounde, Cameroon. The campus is accessible by public transport and situated in a well-connected neighborhood within the city.

## Library

The university library houses a collection of textbooks, academic journals, and reference materials covering computer science, software engineering, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, networking, and general studies. The digital library provides access to online databases such as IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, and JSTOR. The library has quiet study areas, group discussion rooms, and computer terminals for research. Operating hours are Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM and Saturday from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

## Computer Laboratories

ICT University maintains several fully equipped computer labs available to all registered students. Each lab features modern workstations with development tools, IDEs, and software required for coursework. Specialized labs include:

- **Software Development Lab** - Equipped with tools for web, mobile, and desktop application development. Used for SEN and CS program courses such as SEN201 Data Structures & Algorithms and CS201 Object Oriented Programming.
- **Networking Lab** - Features routers, switches, and simulation software for hands-on network configuration. Used for BSc ISN courses including ISN201 Routing & Switching.
- **Cybersecurity Lab** - A sandboxed environment for practicing penetration testing and security analysis. Used for BSc CYS courses including CYS301 Ethical Hacking.
- **AI and Data Science Lab** - Equipped with GPU workstations for machine learning and deep learning projects. Used for BSc AI courses including AI201 Machine Learning Fundamentals.
- **General Access Lab** - Open-access computers for assignments, research, and printing.

Lab assistants are available during operating hours to help students with technical issues.

## Student Center

The student center is the social hub of campus life. It includes lounge areas, a notice board for campus events, and meeting rooms that student organizations can reserve. The center hosts orientation activities, social gatherings, and informal networking events throughout the academic year.

## Cafeteria

The campus cafeteria serves breakfast, lunch, and snacks during the academic day. The menu includes a variety of Cameroonian dishes as well as international options. Meal plans are available for students who prefer regular dining arrangements. A small kiosk near the student center also sells beverages and light snacks.

## Health Center

A basic health center is located on campus and provides first aid, minor medical consultations, and health screenings. The center is staffed by a nurse during weekday hours. Students requiring more comprehensive care are referred to partner hospitals in Yaounde. The health center also coordinates health awareness campaigns on topics such as malaria prevention, nutrition, and mental health.

## Additional Facilities

- **Lecture Halls** - Several air-conditioned halls equipped with projectors and sound systems
- **Wi-Fi** - Campus-wide wireless internet is available to all students and staff
- **Parking** - A secured parking area is provided for students and staff with vehicles
- **Generator Backup** - The campus has backup power to ensure continuity during outages
