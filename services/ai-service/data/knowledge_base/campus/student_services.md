# Student Services at ICT University

## Counseling Services

ICT University provides confidential counseling services to all enrolled students. The counseling office supports students dealing with academic stress, personal challenges, adjustment difficulties, and emotional well-being. Trained counselors are available for individual sessions, and appointments can be booked through the student services office or by email. Walk-in hours are available on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM.

The MindBridge platform is also available 24/7 for students seeking mental health support, providing AI-assisted guidance and crisis resources.

## Career Guidance and Placement

The Career Services Center helps students prepare for professional life. Services include:

- **Resume and CV writing workshops** held monthly
- **Mock interview sessions** with industry professionals
- **Job and internship listings** posted on the career portal
- **Career fairs** organized once each semester, featuring local and international employers
- **Alumni networking events** connecting current students with graduates in the workforce

Students are encouraged to visit the career center starting from their second year to begin planning their professional trajectory.

## Tutoring and Academic Support

The Academic Support Unit offers peer tutoring in high-demand subjects such as programming (CS101, CS201), mathematics (MAT101, MAT201), and networking (ISN101). Tutoring sessions are free of charge and are scheduled based on student demand. Group study sessions led by senior students are also organized before midterm and final examinations.

## Student Records and Registration

The Registrar's Office manages student enrollment, transcripts, certificates, and academic records. Students can access their grades, course schedules, and registration status through the student information system. The office is open Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

## Financial Aid and Scholarships

ICT University offers a limited number of merit-based scholarships for outstanding academic performance. Need-based financial assistance is also available. Students can inquire about payment plans and financial aid options at the Finance Office. Scholarship applications are typically due at the beginning of each academic year (by September 1).

## International Student Support

International students receive assistance with visa documentation, orientation, housing recommendations, and cultural adjustment. A dedicated international student coordinator is available to address concerns and connect students with relevant resources. As a university in Yaounde, Cameroon, ICTU welcomes students from across Central Africa and beyond.
