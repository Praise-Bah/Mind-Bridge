# Student Life at ICT University

## Campus Culture

Student life at ICT University in Yaounde, Cameroon is dynamic and community-oriented. The university encourages students to balance academic rigor with personal growth, social engagement, and extracurricular involvement. The campus community is diverse, bringing together students from across Cameroon and other African countries.

## Student Clubs and Organizations

A variety of student-led clubs and organizations are active on campus:

- **Google Developer Student Club (GDSC)** - Focuses on Google technologies, hosts workshops, hackathons, and coding challenges
- **Cybersecurity Club** - Organizes capture-the-flag competitions and security awareness sessions, often supported by CYS department faculty such as Mr. Ambe Sinclair
- **Entrepreneurship Club** - Supports student startups, hosts pitch nights, and connects members with mentors
- **Debate and Public Speaking Society** - Builds communication skills through structured debates and oratory contests
- **Community Service Club** - Coordinates volunteer initiatives, digital literacy programs in local schools, and outreach events
- **Cultural Association** - Celebrates the diverse cultural heritage of students through festivals, food fairs, and traditional performances
- **Women in Tech** - Promotes gender inclusion in technology fields through mentorship, networking, and skill-building workshops
- **AI Research Club** - Explores artificial intelligence projects and paper discussions, supported by Dr. Yaya Hamidou and Mr. Awa Terence

Students interested in forming a new club can apply through the Dean of Students office with a minimum of ten founding members and a faculty advisor.

## Sports and Recreation

The university supports recreational sports activities including football (soccer), basketball, table tennis, and volleyball. Intramural tournaments are organized each semester, and students can represent the university in inter-university competitions. Sports equipment is available for use through the student center.

## Campus Events

Throughout the academic year, the university hosts events that enrich student life:

- **Orientation Week** - Held at the start of each fall semester (September) to welcome new students, introduce campus resources, and build community
- **Tech Week** - An annual event featuring guest speakers from the tech industry, project exhibitions, and coding marathons
- **Career Fair** - Connects students with employers offering internships and graduate positions
- **Cultural Day** - A celebration of Cameroon's diverse ethnic groups with music, dance, food, and traditional attire
- **End-of-Year Gala** - A formal event marking the close of the academic year with awards and entertainment

## Student Government

The Student Government Association (SGA) represents the student body in university governance. Elected representatives advocate for student interests, organize campus-wide events, and serve as a communication bridge between students and administration. Elections are held annually and all enrolled students are eligible to vote and run for office.

## Housing

ICT University does not operate on-campus dormitories but assists students in finding suitable accommodation in the surrounding neighborhoods in Yaounde. The student services office maintains a list of verified rental options and can help connect students with landlords.

## Mental Health and Wellbeing

ICT University partners with MindBridge to provide 24/7 mental health support to students. The MindBridge platform offers AI-assisted counseling, crisis resources, and wellness tools. Students can access MindBridge through the app or website at any time. In-person counseling is also available through the campus Counseling Center.
