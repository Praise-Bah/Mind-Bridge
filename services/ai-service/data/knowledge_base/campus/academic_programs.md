# Academic Programs at ICT University

## Undergraduate Programs

### BSc in Software Engineering
The Software Engineering program prepares students to design, develop, test, and maintain software systems. Core courses include Object-Oriented Programming, Software Architecture, Database Systems, Web Development, Mobile Application Development, and Agile Project Management. Students complete a capstone project in their final year, typically involving a real-world client.

### BSc in Computer Science
The Computer Science program provides a strong foundation in algorithms, data structures, operating systems, and computational theory. Students also study artificial intelligence, machine learning, and computer graphics. This program is ideal for students interested in research or advanced technical roles in the technology industry.

### BSc in Information Systems
This program bridges business and technology. Students learn about enterprise systems, business process management, IT governance, and systems analysis. Graduates are prepared for roles such as business analyst, IT project manager, and systems consultant.

### BSc in Networking and Telecommunications
Focused on network design, security, and telecommunications infrastructure, this program covers topics such as routing and switching, wireless networks, VoIP, network security, and cloud infrastructure. Students gain hands-on experience with industry-standard equipment and earn preparation for professional certifications like CCNA.

## Postgraduate Programs

### MSc in Information Technology
This master's program is designed for working professionals and recent graduates seeking advanced expertise. Specialization tracks include cybersecurity, data analytics, and IT management. The program includes a thesis or applied research project.

### MSc in Computer Science
A research-oriented program covering advanced topics in artificial intelligence, distributed computing, and algorithm design. Students work closely with faculty on original research and are encouraged to publish in academic journals.

## Professional Certificates

ICT University also offers short-term professional certificate programs in:

- **Cybersecurity Fundamentals** (3 months)
- **Data Science and Analytics** (4 months)
- **Cloud Computing with AWS and Azure** (3 months)
- **Full-Stack Web Development** (6 months)

## Program Duration

Undergraduate programs typically run for three to four years depending on the degree track. Master's programs require one and a half to two years of study. Professional certificates vary from three to six months.

## Teaching Approach

All programs emphasize practical, project-based learning. Students work on group projects, internships, and industry partnerships throughout their studies. The university maintains computer laboratories and development environments to support hands-on coursework.
