# Academic Programs at ICT University

## Undergraduate Programs (BSc)

### BSc in Information & Communication Technology (BSc ICT)
The BSc ICT program provides a broad foundation in information and communication technology, covering digital literacy, systems administration, IT project management, and emerging technologies. Students gain practical skills in managing IT infrastructure and supporting digital transformation in organizations.

Key courses include:
- **ICT101**: Digital Literacy
- **ICT201**: IT Project Management
- **ICT301**: Enterprise Systems
- **ICT401**: ICT Capstone Project

### BSc in Computer Science (BSc CS)
The Computer Science program provides a strong foundation in algorithms, data structures, operating systems, and computational theory. Students study programming, artificial intelligence, machine learning, and database systems. This program is ideal for students interested in research or advanced technical roles in the technology industry.

Key courses include:
- **CS101**: Programming Fundamentals
- **CS201**: Object Oriented Programming
- **CS301**: Database Management Systems
- **CS401**: Artificial Intelligence

### BSc in Software Engineering (BSc SEN)
The Software Engineering program prepares students to design, develop, test, and maintain software systems. The curriculum covers the full software development lifecycle, including requirements analysis, software architecture, implementation, testing, and deployment. Students complete a capstone project in their final year, typically involving a real-world client.

Key courses include:
- **SEN101**: Introduction to Software Engineering
- **SEN201**: Data Structures & Algorithms
- **SEN301**: Software Architecture & Design
- **SEN401**: Capstone Project

### BSc in Cyber Security (BSc CYS)
The Cyber Security program trains students to protect information systems and networks from cyber threats. Students learn about network security, ethical hacking, digital forensics, cryptography, and security policy development. Graduates are prepared for roles in security operations, penetration testing, and compliance.

Key courses include:
- **CYS201**: Network Security
- **CYS301**: Ethical Hacking
- **CYS401**: Digital Forensics & Incident Response

### BSc in Information Systems & Networking (BSc ISN)
This program bridges information systems management with network engineering. Students learn about enterprise systems, network design, routing and switching, wireless networks, cloud infrastructure, and telecommunications. Graduates are prepared for roles such as network administrator, systems analyst, and IT consultant.

Key courses include:
- **ISN101**: Introduction to Networking
- **ISN201**: Routing & Switching
- **ISN301**: Cloud Infrastructure & Services

### BSc in Artificial Intelligence (BSc AI)
The Artificial Intelligence program focuses on machine learning, deep learning, natural language processing, computer vision, and intelligent systems. Students develop strong foundations in mathematics and programming alongside specialized AI coursework. This program prepares graduates for careers in data science, AI engineering, and research.

Key courses include:
- **AI101**: Introduction to Artificial Intelligence
- **AI201**: Machine Learning Fundamentals
- **AI301**: Deep Learning & Neural Networks
- **AI401**: AI Capstone Project

## Cross-Program Foundation Courses

All undergraduate programs share foundation courses including:
- **MAT101**: Discrete Mathematics
- **MAT201**: Linear Algebra & Statistics
- **CS101**: Programming Fundamentals
- **ICT101**: Digital Literacy

## Postgraduate Programs (MSc)

### MSc in Computer Science
A research-oriented program covering advanced topics in artificial intelligence, distributed computing, and algorithm design. Students work closely with faculty on original research and are encouraged to publish in academic journals.

### MSc in Information Technology
This master's program is designed for working professionals and recent graduates seeking advanced expertise. Specialization tracks include cybersecurity, data analytics, and IT management. The program includes a thesis or applied research project.

### MSc in Software Engineering
An advanced program focusing on software architecture, advanced software project management, and research methodologies in software engineering. Students undertake a thesis or applied project.

## Program Duration

Undergraduate programs typically run for three to four years depending on the degree track. Master's programs require one and a half to two years of study.

## Teaching Approach

All programs emphasize practical, project-based learning. Students work on group projects, internships, and industry partnerships throughout their studies. The university maintains computer laboratories and development environments to support hands-on coursework.
