# Campus Wellness and Mental Health Resources

## Commitment to Student Well-Being

ICT University recognizes that academic success is closely linked to mental and emotional well-being. The university is committed to creating a supportive environment where students can seek help without stigma and access the resources they need to thrive.

## Counseling Center

The Campus Counseling Center provides free, confidential mental health support to all registered students. Services include:

- **Individual counseling** for anxiety, depression, stress management, relationship issues, and adjustment challenges
- **Group counseling sessions** on topics such as managing academic pressure, building resilience, and coping with grief
- **Crisis intervention** for students in immediate distress
- **Referrals** to external mental health professionals and hospitals when specialized care is needed

The counseling center is located in the student services building and is open Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Appointments can be made by visiting the office, calling the front desk, or sending an email. Walk-in consultations are available but subject to counselor availability.

## Peer Support Program

ICT University operates a trained peer support network. Selected students receive training in active listening, mental health first aid, and referral protocols. Peer supporters are available to provide a first point of contact for students who may not be ready to visit a professional counselor. Peer supporters can be identified by their designated badges and are present at student events and in common areas.

## MindBridge Integration

The university has partnered with MindBridge, a digital mental health platform, to extend wellness support beyond the physical counseling center. Through MindBridge, students can:

- **Access an AI-powered chatbot** for immediate emotional support and guided self-help exercises
- **Complete mental health self-assessments** to better understand their well-being
- **Connect with professional counselors** for virtual sessions
- **Access educational resources** on stress management, mindfulness, sleep hygiene, and healthy relationships
- **Track their mood and well-being** over time using the platform's journaling and tracking features

MindBridge is available 24/7 through the web and mobile applications, making support accessible even outside of counseling center hours. All interactions on the platform are confidential and comply with data privacy standards.

## Wellness Programs

The university organizes regular wellness initiatives including:

- **Mental Health Awareness Week** held once per semester with workshops, guest speakers, and screening events
- **Stress Management Workshops** before examination periods
- **Mindfulness and Meditation Sessions** offered weekly in the student center
- **Physical Fitness Activities** organized in partnership with the sports department

## Emergency Support

Students experiencing a mental health crisis can contact the counseling center during office hours or reach the university's emergency line after hours. In cases of immediate danger, students are advised to contact local emergency services or proceed to the nearest hospital emergency department.
