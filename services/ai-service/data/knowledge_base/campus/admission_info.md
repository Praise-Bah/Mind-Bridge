# Admission Information - ICT University

## Undergraduate Admission Requirements

To be eligible for admission to an undergraduate program at ICT University, applicants must meet the following requirements:

- **GCE Advanced Level** with at least two passes, including one in a science or mathematics subject, or
- **Baccalauréat** (French system) with a scientific or technical specialization, or
- **Equivalent international qualification** recognized by the Ministry of Higher Education of Cameroon
- A minimum grade average as specified by the department of the intended program
- Proficiency in English (French-speaking applicants may be required to pass an English proficiency assessment)

## Postgraduate Admission Requirements

- A Bachelor's degree in a relevant field from an accredited institution
- Minimum GPA of 2.5 out of 4.0 or equivalent
- Two academic or professional reference letters
- A statement of purpose outlining research interests and career goals
- Relevant professional experience is an advantage but not mandatory

## Application Process

1. **Obtain the application form** from the Admissions Office on campus or download it from the university website
2. **Complete the form** with accurate personal, academic, and contact information
3. **Attach required documents**: certified copies of academic transcripts, certificates, passport-sized photographs, and a copy of a valid national ID or passport
4. **Submit the application** along with the non-refundable application fee
5. **Await the admission decision**, which is typically communicated within two to four weeks via email or SMS

## Tuition and Fees

Tuition fees are quoted in Central African CFA Francs (FCFA). Approximate costs per academic year are as follows:

| Program | Annual Tuition (FCFA) |
|---|---|
| BSc Software Engineering | 800,000 - 1,200,000 |
| BSc Computer Science | 800,000 - 1,200,000 |
| BSc Information Systems | 750,000 - 1,100,000 |
| BSc Networking | 800,000 - 1,200,000 |
| MSc Programs | 1,000,000 - 1,500,000 |
| Professional Certificates | 300,000 - 600,000 |

Additional fees include registration (50,000 FCFA), laboratory fees (25,000 FCFA per semester), and student association dues (10,000 FCFA per year). Fees are subject to change and students should confirm current rates with the Finance Office.

## Payment Options

Tuition may be paid in full at the beginning of the year or in installments across the two semesters. Payment is accepted via bank transfer, mobile money (MTN Mobile Money, Orange Money), or cash at the Finance Office.

## Application Deadlines

- **First intake (September)**: Applications close by August 15
- **Second intake (January)**: Applications close by December 15
- Late applications may be considered on a case-by-case basis subject to available slots
