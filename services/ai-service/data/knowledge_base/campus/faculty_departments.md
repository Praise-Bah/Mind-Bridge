# Faculty and Departments - ICT University

## Faculty Structure

ICT University is organized into academic departments, each led by a Head of Department (HOD) who reports to the Dean of Faculty. The faculty is composed of full-time lecturers, part-time instructors, and visiting professors from industry and partner institutions.

## Department of Software Engineering

The Department of Software Engineering focuses on teaching students to build reliable, scalable, and maintainable software systems. The curriculum covers the full software development lifecycle, including requirements analysis, design, implementation, testing, and deployment.

**Faculty members:**
- **Dr. Djomadji Eric** - Software Architecture, Software Design Patterns, Capstone Project supervision
- **Mr. Fondze Nfor** - Introduction to Software Engineering, Agile Methodologies
- **Mr. Nkemeni Peter** - Data Structures & Algorithms, Full-Stack Development
- **Mr. Tamunjoh Emmanuel** - Software Testing & Quality Assurance, DevOps

Key courses: SEN101 Introduction to Software Engineering, SEN201 Data Structures & Algorithms, SEN301 Software Architecture & Design, SEN401 Capstone Project.

## Department of Computer Science

This department provides the theoretical and mathematical foundations of computing. Faculty research interests include artificial intelligence, machine learning, algorithm optimization, and computational complexity. The department maintains strong ties with research institutions and encourages students to participate in academic conferences and competitions.

**Faculty members:**

- **Dr. Tchangai Martial** - Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning
- **Dr. Fouda Jean** - Operating Systems, Computer Architecture

The Cyber Security department trains students to protect information systems and networks from threats. Faculty members hold professional certifications and bring industry experience in penetration testing, digital forensics, and security governance.

## Faculty Qualifications

All full-time lecturers hold at least a Master's degree in their area of specialization, with many holding doctoral degrees (PhD). Part-time instructors are typically working professionals who bring current industry knowledge into the classroom. The university supports faculty development through conference attendance, research grants, and professional certification sponsorship.

## Office Hours

Faculty members maintain regular office hours for student consultations, typically two to three hours per week. Office hour schedules are posted on department notice boards and communicated at the beginning of each semester. Students are encouraged to schedule appointments in advance via email.
