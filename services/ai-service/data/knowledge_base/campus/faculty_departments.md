# Faculty and Departments - ICT University

## Faculty Structure

ICT University is organized into academic departments, each led by a Head of Department (HOD) who reports to the Dean of Faculty. The faculty is composed of full-time lecturers, part-time instructors, and visiting professors from industry and partner institutions.

## Department of Software Engineering

The Department of Software Engineering focuses on teaching students to build reliable, scalable, and maintainable software systems. The curriculum covers the full software development lifecycle, including requirements analysis, design, implementation, testing, and deployment. Faculty members in this department have expertise in web technologies, mobile development, DevOps, and software project management.

Key courses offered: Software Design Patterns, Agile Methodologies, Full-Stack Development, Software Testing and Quality Assurance, and Human-Computer Interaction.

## Department of Computer Science

This department provides the theoretical and mathematical foundations of computing. Faculty research interests include artificial intelligence, machine learning, algorithm optimization, and computational complexity. The department maintains strong ties with research institutions and encourages students to participate in academic conferences and competitions.

Key courses offered: Data Structures and Algorithms, Operating Systems, Compiler Design, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Computer Architecture.

## Department of Information Technology and Information Systems

The IT and Information Systems department prepares students for careers at the intersection of business and technology. Faculty expertise spans enterprise resource planning, IT governance, database administration, and digital transformation. The department works closely with local businesses to provide students with internship and project opportunities.

Key courses offered: Database Management Systems, IT Project Management, Business Intelligence, Systems Analysis and Design, and E-Commerce.

## Department of Networking and Telecommunications

This department trains students in the design, configuration, and management of computer networks and telecommunications infrastructure. Faculty members hold professional certifications (CCNA, CCNP, CompTIA) and bring practical industry experience to the classroom. The department operates a dedicated networking laboratory.

Key courses offered: Computer Networks, Network Security, Wireless Communications, Cloud Infrastructure, and Telecommunications Systems.

## Faculty Qualifications

All full-time lecturers hold at least a Master's degree in their area of specialization, with many holding doctoral degrees. Part-time instructors are typically working professionals who bring current industry knowledge into the classroom. The university supports faculty development through conference attendance, research grants, and professional certification sponsorship.

## Office Hours

Faculty members maintain regular office hours for student consultations, typically two to three hours per week. Office hour schedules are posted on department notice boards and communicated at the beginning of each semester. Students are encouraged to schedule appointments in advance via email.
